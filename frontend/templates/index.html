Our Healthcare Services

From digital booking to bedside care, CarePlus Hospital integrates all aspects of your clinical experience under one unified platform.

Online Appointment Booking

Patients can browse real-time doctor availability calendars, choose precise 30-minute consultation slots, receive automatic token numbers, and manage bookings 24/7 without waiting on phone holds.

  • Real-time slot conflict prevention (guaranteed zero double-booking)
  • Instant SMS & in-app confirmation notifications
  • Easy self-service cancellation and reschedule requests

Specialist Doctor Consultation

Access in-depth consultations with senior physicians and surgeons. Our clinical suites feature private examination rooms, immediate point-of-care diagnostics, and clear treatment counseling.

  • Dedicated 30-minute doctor-patient consultation time
  • Digital prescription generation with standardized dosages
  • Follow-up appointment scheduling & clinical tracking

Unified Patient Management

Experience centralized health records management. Track appointment histories, download digital prescriptions, monitor ongoing medical advice, and access billing statements with ease.

  • Secure, encrypted patient health profile and history
  • Transparent billing invoices with cash, card, and online checkout
  • Front-desk receptionist walk-in token generation

24/7 Healthcare & Emergency Support

Our dedicated support desk and emergency medical teams operate around the clock. Whether you need immediate trauma support or clinical guidance, our specialists are ready to respond.

  • Dedicated 24/7 emergency trauma hotline: +1 (800) 555-CARE
  • Rapid ambulance dispatch and trauma triage center
  • Patient assistance desk for insurance and billing queries
